Privacy Policy

Flow State: Engineering ("the app") is made by an independent developer. This policy explains what happens with your data when you play.

We don't collect anything

The app has no analytics, no advertising, and no account or sign-up. It doesn't send your gameplay, device identifiers, or personal information to us or to any third party.

What stays on your device

Your save file (factory layout, progress, settings) is stored locally on your device only. It never leaves your phone unless you delete the app or use Apple's own device backup/transfer tools.

Apple Game Center

If you sign in to Game Center, your leaderboard scores (best production rate, rockets launched) are submitted to Apple's Game Center service so you can see rankings. That is handled entirely by Apple under Apple's own privacy policy — we never see this data ourselves.

Photos

If you choose to save a screenshot of your factory, the app asks for permission to write a single image to your Photos library. Nothing is read from your library, and nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Notifications

The app may schedule a local reminder notification (e.g. "your trucks are full") entirely on your device. No notification data is sent to us or anyone else.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the update will be posted on this page.
